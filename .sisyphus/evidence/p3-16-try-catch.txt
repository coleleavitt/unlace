Design:
  - structure_with_landing_pads(func, landing_pad_addrs) is the entry point.
  - It calls structure() first, then post-processes the HIR tree.
  - resolve_landing_pad_addrs() confirms which landing pad addresses match
    actual block first-instruction addresses in the function.
  - wrap_try_catch() scans HIR nodes at each level; when a node's address
    matches a landing pad, all preceding nodes become the try_body and the
    landing pad node(s) become the catch_body.
  - Recursion handles landing pads inside nested constructs (if/while/etc).
  - When landing_pad_addrs is empty, behavior is identical to structure().
  - Uses BTreeSet exclusively (no HashMap).
