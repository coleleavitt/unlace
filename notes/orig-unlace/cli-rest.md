# Original unlace CLI-side techniques vs main

Read-only comparison of the CLI-side modules on `origin/master` (the archived engine) against current `main`:
`quality.rs`, `batch.rs`, `parallel.rs`, `cache.rs`, `identify_packer.rs`, `project.rs`, `args.rs`, `main.rs`, and the
`unlace-sbom` crate. Nothing was built or run. Techniques are described in our own words. Out of scope by owner direction and
not listed: MCP, debugger, emulator, LSP, and hand-written lifters. Built on `notes/archived-master-inventory.md`, which already
covers the crate-level rows; this note adds the module-level detail and the flag-level commands that inventory did not list
(`patch`, `sigload`, `tilload`, `symfetch`, `config show`).

Paths on `origin/master` are under `crates/unlace-cli/src/` unless shown. Main paths are relative to the repo root.

## Notes for the lead

- Row 1 is the highest-value item for real use: whole-binary project export has no resume today.
- Rows 2 and 3 are cheap and cover gaps the ground-truth harness cannot see (binaries with no DWARF).
- Row 9 is listed so nobody ports `cache.rs` without the option-set fix. Master never wired it.
- Row 11 and the `DiskCache` in row 9 have no callers on master, so porting them adds no tested behaviour.
- Rows 5 and 6 are not in `archived-master-inventory.md`. Row 8 and the `config show` part of row 10 are not there either.
- Existing issues on the repo: #1217 (row 3), #1212 (release checksums, row 7's home), #1213 (fuzz), #1214 (anti-debug), #1215 (plugin SDK). Rows 1, 2 and 6 had no open or closed issue when searched.
- Filed from this note: rows 1, 2 and 6 (see the PR body for issue numbers). Rows 5, 7, 9, 10 are left unfiled on purpose.

Not checked: whether main's SLEIGH userop indices match master's tag ranges (row 4); whether a workspace SBOM tool is already in main's CI tooling (row 7).
