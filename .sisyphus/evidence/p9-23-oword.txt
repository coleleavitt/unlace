Type mappings (c_type / ida_ptr_type / SUBPIECE casts):

  PrimitiveType::Int(16)         -> __int128 (gcc/clang built-in)
  ida_ptr_type(16)               -> _OWORD *
  SUBPIECE cast for 16 bytes     -> (_OWORD)
