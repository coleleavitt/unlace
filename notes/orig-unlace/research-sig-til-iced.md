Scope: FLIRT `.sig` and IDA `.til` handling, the iced x86 usage, and the `research/ida-sig-til-samples` and `research/iced-ecosystem` mirrors. Original side is `origin/master` (archived, read with `git show`). Current side is `origin/main` at 188480126a. Read first: `notes/archived-master-inventory.md` and `notes/ida-gap/types-plugins-sigs.md`. Nothing was built or run.

Status words: **have** (main does it), **partial**, **missing**, **covered differently** (main reaches the same result another way), **dead on origin** (no caller outside its own module).

## Recommendation

Two issues, for the rows worth porting: row 1 (host-generated C runtime packs) and row 2 (branch displacements without relocations). Rows 3 to 16 are have, covered differently, dead on origin, or not worth porting. Three issues would need a third item that qualifies, and none does.

## Notes on the inputs

- `research/iced-ecosystem/` is a mirror of the iced GUI framework and its widgets (`iced`, `iced_aw`, `iced-code-editor`, `iced_term`, `halloy`, and others). Its README calls it the reference for the desktop UI (Batch 28). It contains no iced-x86 source and no decompiler technique, so it gives no rows. If iced-x86 was meant, that source is not in the mirror.
- `research/ida-sig-til-samples/`: 162 `.sig` files (155 under `sig_pc`, 7 under `sig_arm`) with format version bytes 5 (73 files), 6 (2), 7 (30) and 10 (57). All fall in the 5 to 11 range that `flirt-sig` 0.2.1 reads. 102 `.til` files (79 `til_pc`, 23 `til_arm`), all with the `IDATIL` magic. The TILs include macOS 11 to 14 arm64, iPhone OS 12 to 17, xnu, Android, gnulnx, uefi64 and wince. These are the targets row 7 covers once `UNLACE_TIL_DIR` is set.
- The `unlace-wt-main` checkout is not at `origin/main`, so every main citation here uses `git show` or `git grep` against `origin/main`.
