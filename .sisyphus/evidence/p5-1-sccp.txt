P5-1: Enable SCCP in optimization pipeline
============================================

Change: Added SccpPass to the fixedpoint optimization loop in pipeline.rs

Before:
  let passes: &[&dyn Pass] = &[&fold, &prop, &dce];

After:
  let passes: &[&dyn Pass] = &[&fold, &prop, &dce, &sccp];

Imports added: SccpPass

Test results:
  Baseline: 995 passed, 0 failed
  After SCCP: 995 passed, 0 failed
  No regressions.

SCCP (Sparse Conditional Constant Propagation) benefits from iteration because
constants discovered by SCCP can enable further constant folding and copy
propagation, which in turn can expose more SCCP opportunities.
