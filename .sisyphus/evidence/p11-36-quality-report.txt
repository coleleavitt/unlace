5. STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS
-----------------------
medium.c's 13 gotos remain the primary parity bottleneck. The scoring
formula penalizes -2.0 per goto, so 13 gotos = -26.0 penalty which
exceeds the maximum possible control-flow credit (11.0), yielding 0.0.

The gotos are genuinely irreducible:
  - main: 4 gotos from multi-entry loop SCCs
  - f_rec_fib: 2 gotos from recursive fibonacci pattern
  - f_rec_power.constprop.0: 7 gotos from constprop-created irreducible cluster

These cannot be eliminated without either:
  a) Node splitting (code duplication) which risks exponential blowup
  b) Controlled duplication with size budgets (Phase 12 candidate)
