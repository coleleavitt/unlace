# Hex-Rays and structuring techniques: original unlace (origin/master) vs main

Scope: the notes in `research/` on Hex-Rays internals and structuring (`hexrays-algorithm-map.md`, `hexrays-algorithms-deep.md`, `hexrays-exception-structuring.md`, `hexrays-lvar-coalescing.md`, `irreducible-cfg-2026-05.md`, `structuring-and-temps-research.md`, `prologue_sp_notes.md`), compared with the archived engine on origin/master (3aaf121866) and with main (517a594ef9). Techniques are described in our own words. Nothing was built or run.

Status words: **Have** means main has the same technique (checked by file existence, grep and wiring, not by running it). **Partial** means main has part of it. **Missing** means it is not on main.

## Summary

- Most of the Hex-Rays-inspired work on origin/master was superseded on main. Main's lvar merge is a port of Ghidra's `merge.cc`, and main's structurer has the goto-quality family (ifelseflatten, earlyreturn, gotoreduce, gotosink, crossjumpreverter, and more).
- Five master passes are live and have no counterpart on main: irreducible node splitting, if-chain to switch, cmov-cascade to switch, redundant-condition normalization, and loop-exit merge. Master also runs DREAM reaching-condition refinement on residual irreducible regions. Main has no reaching-condition code.
- Two gaps are on both trees: Itanium `throw` printing and the per-clause catch split. The try/catch recovery itself is on both trees.
- Master's LICM, string-hash switch, temp-home and prologue passes are judgement calls (rows 7, 9, 10 and 11 below).
