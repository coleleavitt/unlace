P4-14: Switch case fall-through support
=======================================

Changes:
- Extended switch case tuple from (SwitchCase, Vec<HirNode>) to (SwitchCase, Vec<HirNode>, bool)
  where the bool is `fall_through`
- Updated ALL 8 locations that construct or destructure switch cases:
  - hir.rs: type definition
  - structurer.rs: try_structure_switch, wrap_try_catch, recover_for_loops_in_children,
    node_references_varnode
  - if_to_switch.rs: rewrite_node (2 locations)
  - switch_recovery.rs: walk_node
  - ternary_recovery.rs: recover_node
- Structurer: detect fall-through when consecutive switch targets share the same BlockId
  - Fall-through cases get empty body and fall_through=true
  - Non-fall-through cases get structured body and fall_through=false
- C printer: fall-through cases emit `/* fall through */` comment instead of `break;`
- 2 new tests:
  - test_switch_normal_has_break: non-fallthrough cases emit break;
  - test_switch_fallthrough_no_break: fall-through case has comment, no break;

Test results: 975 tests, 0 failures (up from 973 after P4-13)
No regressions.
