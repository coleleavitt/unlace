P5-13: ROL/ROR rotation instructions
=====================================

Commit: feat(lift-x86): lift ROL/ROR rotation instructions

Files changed:
- crates/unlace-lift-x86/src/ops/bitwise.rs: Added lift_rol() and lift_ror()
- crates/unlace-lift-x86/src/lift.rs: Added match arms for Rol, Ror
- crates/unlace-lift-x86/src/lib.rs: Added 2 tests

Implementation:
- ROL: masked = count & (bits-1); left = INT_LEFT(a, masked); complement = INT_SUB(bits, masked); right = INT_RIGHT(a, complement); result = INT_OR(left, right)
- ROR: masked = count & (bits-1); right = INT_RIGHT(a, masked); complement = INT_SUB(bits, masked); left = INT_LEFT(a, complement); result = INT_OR(right, left)
- Both mask the shift count like SHL/SHR/SAR

- test_ror_reg_imm: 48 C1 C8 04 = ror rax, 4 (verifies IntRight, IntLeft, IntOr)
