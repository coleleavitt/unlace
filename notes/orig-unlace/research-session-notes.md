# Techniques from the original unlace planning notes that never landed on main

Scope: the original checkout's planning and research notes (`ida-notes`, `HANDOFF.md`, `.sisyphus/` plans and notepads, `research/*.md`, and the `research/opencode-sessions` exports, read by keyword scan) checked against the archived research decompiler at `origin/master` and against `origin/main`. Sections marked "already recorded" appear in `notes/ida-gap/`, so they are cross-referenced, not repeated as new findings. Method: each technique was located in `origin/master` by name (git grep), then checked in `origin/main` by name and by the code it would touch.

Not read in full: the PDFs in `research/papers/` (only the extracted notes that cite them), and the two large opencode transcripts (keyword scan only). `notes/archived-master-inventory.md` does not exist on `origin/main` in this checkout, so it was not compared. `HANDOFF.md` tracks the current crate split (steps 6 to 9), not missing techniques, so it contributes nothing to the table. Out of scope and excluded: debugger, emulator, MCP/LSP, and wholesale hand-written lifters.

## Not worth porting (decided in the notes)

- Full DREAM reaching-condition structuring. The research note itself calls it a core rewrite with high blast radius.
- SSE scalar folding (P15C-2 in the phase 15 plan). It needs a GCC vectorization pattern database.
- dewolf readability metrics. These are survey results, not a technique.
- The Hex-Rays debug and dump printers (`sub_254AD0`, `sub_3AE9C0`, from `ida-notes`). They are internal and never produce C output.
- The invsign display rule (`is_invsign`, from `ida-notes`). It is a one-line display tweak that is not on any open list.
