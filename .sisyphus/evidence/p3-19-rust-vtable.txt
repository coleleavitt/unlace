### New file: crates/unlace-passes/src/rust_vtable.rs
- `RustVtablePass` implementing `Pass` trait
- Detects fat pointer pattern: CALLIND(LOAD(LOAD(base + 8) + N))
- Annotates vtable dispatches with slot index = N/8
- Supports INT_ADD and PTRADD for offset resolution
