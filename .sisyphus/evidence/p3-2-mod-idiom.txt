P3-2: Compiler modulo idiom recovery
=====================================

TASK
----
Recognize compiler-emitted modulo patterns in the post-SSA IR and rewrite
them back to INT_REM / INT_SREM. Operates AFTER P3-1 division_idiom
(which canonicalises INT_DIV / INT_SDIV from multiply-shift form), so the
remaining modulo patterns appear as clean ALU chains.

------------

- crates/unlace-passes/src/lib.rs                    (modified: +2 lines)
  * `pub mod modulo_idiom;`
  * `pub use modulo_idiom::ModuloIdiomPass;`

PATTERNS RECOGNISED
-------------------
1. Unsigned subtract form
     INT_SUB(v, INT_MULT(INT_DIV(v, N),  N))  ->  INT_REM(v, N)
2. Signed subtract form
     INT_SUB(v, INT_MULT(INT_SDIV(v, N), N))  ->  INT_SREM(v, N)
3. Power-of-two AND mask (only in index-like context)
     INT_AND(v, 2^k - 1)  ->  INT_REM(v, 2^k)
   * "Index-like" heuristic: AND output is consumed by INT_ADD,
     INT_LESS, or INT_LESSEQUAL in the same block.

ALGORITHM SKETCH
----------------
Per block, two linear sweeps:

* Sweep 1 (subtract form)
  - Find every IntSub.
  - Look back for the producer of inputs[1]; require IntMult.
  - From the IntMult, extract (variable, constant_M) commutatively.
  - Look back for the producer of `variable`; require IntDiv or IntSdiv
    with inputs == [SUB.inputs[0], constant_N].
  - Require constant_N == constant_M (modulus must equal divisor).
  - Replace IntSub with IntRem / IntSrem; neutralize the original
    INT_MULT and INT_DIV / INT_SDIV to COPY so DCE can drop them.

* Sweep 2 (power-of-two AND)
  - Find every IntAnd whose constant operand is one of
    { 0x7, 0xF, 0x1F, 0x3F, 0x7F, 0xFF, 0xFFFF,
      0x7FFFFFFF, 0x7FFFFFFFFFFFFFFF }.
  - Verify mask + 1 is_power_of_two (defence in depth).
  - Walk downstream insns in the same block; only rewrite if the
    AND output is consumed by IntAdd, IntLess, or IntLessequal.
  - Replace AND with IntRem(v, 2^k).

DETERMINISM
-----------
- BTreeMap-only (no HashMap, no HashSet) per workspace lint.
- Same matcher style as sibling division_idiom.rs (committed Wave 1).
- Per-block sweeps preserve insn order; no reordering of side effects.
