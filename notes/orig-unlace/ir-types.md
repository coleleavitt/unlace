# Original unlace IR types and data model: archived master vs main

Scope: `crates/unlace-ir/src` on `origin/master` (archived engine at 3aaf121866): `tinfo.rs`, `types.rs`, `signature.rs`, `lvar.rs`, `xref.rs`, `data_xrefs.rs`, plus the rest of the crate (`known_bits`, `memory_ssa`, `alias`, `eh`, `stack`, `mem_object`, `reg_alias`, `syscall`, `verify`, `data_layout`, `arch_support`, `value_lattice`). The SSA, CFG and lifter files (`ssa.rs`, `cfg.rs`, `lifter.rs`) are left to the IR-core note.

Main is a Ghidra-style port (`Funcdata`, `Datatype`, `Varnode`, `CircleRange`, `nzmask`). None of master's IR types exist there under their own names. Each technique below is marked by what main does instead, with file:line from main at 517a594ef9. Status words: **have** (same technique, different shape), **partial**, **missing**. Lines on master are from `git show origin/master:crates/unlace-ir/src/<file>`. Nothing was built or run.

Recommendation: port the top three rows. The rest are either already covered or not worth their cost now.
