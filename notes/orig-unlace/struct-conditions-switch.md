# Structuring, condition and switch techniques on origin/master

Scope: `crates/unlace-struct/src/` on the archived `origin/master` (`switch_recovery.rs`, `if_to_switch.rs`,
`short_circuit.rs`, `condition_refinement.rs`, `reaching_conditions.rs`), compared against current `origin/main`.
Read-only: no code was built or run. Master line numbers are on `origin/master`; main line numbers are on `origin/main`.

Summary: main already covers most of this ground under Ghidra's CollapseStructure and a Phoenix-style region
structurer (default on). Missing: the DREAM reaching-condition engine and its condition-factor refinement. Those are
the only two rows worth porting. Everything else is have, partial, or not worth porting.
