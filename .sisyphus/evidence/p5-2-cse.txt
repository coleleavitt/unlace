P5-2: Enable CSE in optimization pipeline
============================================

Change: Added CsePass to the fixedpoint optimization loop in pipeline.rs

Before:
  let passes: &[&dyn Pass] = &[&fold, &prop, &dce, &sccp];

After:
  let passes: &[&dyn Pass] = &[&fold, &prop, &dce, &sccp, &cse];

Imports added: CsePass

Test results:
  Baseline: 995 passed, 0 failed
  After CSE: 995 passed, 0 failed
  No regressions.

CSE (Common Subexpression Elimination) benefits from iteration because
constant folding and copy propagation can reveal identical expressions,
and CSE-created copies can enable further propagation.
